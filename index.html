YOUR PRIVACY

Privacy, in plain language.

This is a storefront preview. The checkout form does not submit or save the personal details entered into it, and no order or payment is processed.

The shopping bag is saved in your browser’s local storage so it can be shown again on this device. You can remove it by clearing this site’s browser storage.

Product photos are served from the supplier’s image host. Loading those images may share standard connection information with that host. No analytics or advertising features have been added to this storefront.

This notice describes the current preview only; it is not a substitute for a reviewed privacy policy. Before launch, the store owner should update it to reflect the final hosting, payment, analytics and order-processing services.
