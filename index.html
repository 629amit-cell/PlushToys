ABOUT PLUSH STORY

A little joy, chosen with care.

Plush Story is an India-focused plush-toy shop made for thoughtful gifting. Browse teddy bears, animal friends, character cushions and playful little surprises, all in one place.

We’re building this collection to make it easier to find a plush friend for birthdays, celebrations and everyday moments. Product details, sizes and prices are shown on every plush page, with secure payment handled by Razorpay at checkout.

HERE TO HELP

Support & common questions.

Can I place an order?

Yes. Add a plush to your bag and continue to checkout. Payments are processed securely by Razorpay; we never see or store your card details.

How are product prices set?

Size-based prices are shown on each product. Delivery charges are shown at checkout before payment.

How do I ask about a product?

Use the contact details in the Contact section once they’ve been added by the store owner.

What are the return and delivery terms?

Delivery is arranged after payment. Returns and exchanges follow the store policy; the final terms will be published before launch.

YOUR PRIVACY

Privacy, in plain language.

When you check out, we collect your name, email, mobile number and delivery address so we can create and fulfil your order. Payment details are collected and processed by Razorpay; this store does not receive or store your card, UPI or netbanking credentials.

The shopping bag is saved in your browser’s local storage so it can be shown again on this device. You can remove it by clearing this site’s browser storage.

Product photos and catalogue images are served from this site after being downloaded from supplier listings. No analytics or advertising features have been added to this storefront.

Order records may be stored in Cloudflare D1 and Razorpay so the store can process and support your order. This notice is a plain-language summary, not a substitute for a reviewed privacy policy; the store owner should confirm the final hosting, payment, retention and contact details before launch.
